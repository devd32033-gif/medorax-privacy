MedoraX — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 October 2026 · Operated by aacrazy enterprises

1. About this policy

This policy explains what data the MedoraX app stores, where it is kept, and how it is protected. MedoraX is a clinic and hospital management application developed and operated by aacrazy enterprises. It is used by clinics to manage their operations and by patients, through the patient portal, to view their own health records.

2. What data the app stores

Clinic data entered by the clinic itself: patient records, appointments, OPD visits, prescriptions, billing, pharmacy stock, laboratory results, IPD admissions, staff accounts and audit activity. Account details such as name, phone number, email and role are stored to log users in.

Patient portal data: when a patient enrols or verifies their identity, the app stores their full name, phone number and date of birth, together with a PIN they choose to access their own records. The portal then shows the health records the patient's clinic has made available to them, such as visits, prescriptions, medications, allergies, vitals and reports.

Patients only see their own records. Clinic staff only see the records of their own clinic, according to their role.

3. Where the data is stored

By default all data is stored on the clinic's own computer in the app's local database. If cloud sync is configured for the clinic, a copy of the synced data is uploaded to the clinic's private cloud space so that multiple computers, devices and branches stay in sync. That cloud space is controlled by the clinic's account, is isolated per clinic, and is not accessible to other clinics.

4. How the data is protected

  • Backups created by the app are encrypted (AES) with the clinic's own backup password, so a backup file cannot be opened without it.
  • Data transferred for cloud sync is encrypted in transit.
  • The mobile app blocks screenshots and screen recording of sensitive content.
  • Access is controlled by role, and activity is recorded in an audit log.
  • The app does not run advertisements, does not use analytics or tracking, and does not sell or share clinic data with third parties.

5. How long data is kept

Data is kept until the clinic or the patient deletes it. The clinic can reset selected data from Settings → Reset Data, and exported backups can be deleted at any time. Deleting data or deleting a backup is permanent and cannot be undone. Patients can ask their clinic to correct or delete their records at any time.

6. Your responsibility

The clinic is responsible for the patient data it collects and for obtaining any consents or notices required by the laws that apply to the clinic. MedoraX provides tools (encryption, backups, access by role) to help the clinic protect that data. For questions about a personal health record, patients should contact their clinic directly.

7. Children's privacy

The app is used by healthcare providers to manage patient records, which may include minors, under the clinic's responsibility. The app does not knowingly collect data from children outside of this context.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be published at this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

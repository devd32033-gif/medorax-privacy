Delete Your MedoraX Account & Data

MedoraX, by aacrazy enterprises · Effective date: 21 October 2026

How to request account deletion

  1. Email aacrazyenterprises@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request — MedoraX".
  2. In the email, include the username you sign in with (staff: your email address; patient: your registered phone number) so we can locate your account.
  3. We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and then confirm by email when your account is deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your MedoraX login account: username (email or phone number), password/PIN, and account preferences.
  • Your access to the patient portal and any synced session data on your devices.

What is kept, and why

  • Your health records (visits, prescriptions, reports, bills) are controlled by your clinic. MedoraX stores them on behalf of the clinic, and they are deleted only when the clinic instructs us to delete them — ask your clinic to remove your records if you want them deleted.
  • Billing and invoice records may be kept where law requires a retention period.
  • Security and audit logs are kept only for as long as needed for fraud prevention and security.

Retention period

Deletion requests are completed within 30 days of the date we verify your request. Records that must be kept by law are deleted as soon as that legal retention period ends.
